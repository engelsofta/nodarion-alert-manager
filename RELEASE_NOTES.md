# Nodarion Pager 1.1.0

## Your home's drama. Now with better lighting.

The pager has redecorated. A warm interior background, restrained glass surfaces and refined dialogs bring a calmer look to the dashboard, rules, settings and Live Activity wizard. Smaller screens get a more comfortable rule list and dialog layout.

Background updates now preserve your editing focus, scroll position and unsaved settings. Notifications lead with the entity display name and a concise Alarm or OK status. Resolved non-critical alerts clear their phone and persistent notifications, including escalation recipients; critical notifications remain visible.

The README includes new screenshots with fictional demo entities, recipients, readings and history. Notification lifecycle and frontend refresh regression tests accompany this release.

### Installation

Update through HACS or extract `nodarion-alert-manager.zip` into `/config/custom_components/nodarion_pager/`, then restart Home Assistant and refresh the browser.

Requires Home Assistant 2026.7.0 or later. Live Activities additionally require a compatible Companion App/device and a `notify.mobile_app_*` service.

See the [README](https://github.com/engelsofta/nodarion-alert-manager#readme) and [changelog](https://github.com/engelsofta/nodarion-alert-manager/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) for details.
